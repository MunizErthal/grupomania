## Scope
Public site (home, single scrolling page + depot sections) for Grupo Mania D'Água. Visitor mode: Persuade. Hidden admin (/painel) is an Operate surface inheriting this world quietly.

## Audience / job / action
Neighbours in Estância Velha and Novo Hamburgo whose gas or water ran out. Action: Pedir pelo WhatsApp to the right depot, in two taps. Proof: real video/photos of the yard, trucks, team; Supergasbras authorized reseller; 24h self-service machine at Rincão; since 2003.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The site speaks in the markings stamped and printed on what we deliver — the P13 collar stamp, tare weight, numbered seal (lacre), the 20L jug lot label — and refuses the category's flame-and-water-drop hero with a stock product shot.
OWN-WORLD (revised after owner feedback): quiet neutral grounds that alternate only in value (paper #f3f3f1 / white), dark steel #1c2022 for the film, the 24h station and the footer, and a single owner-pinned accent #d33100 for ordering, headline emphasis (last sentence of editable headlines) and small markings. The markings world survives in type: ultra-condensed Archivo black for headlines and stamped codes (P13, 20L, 03.03.2003), tracked micro-caps stamps. Components are soft: white cards with 22px radius and soft shadow, pill buttons and chips, frameless photos with generous radius. No rivets, no hard borders, no ochre, no blue fields.
STORY: Visitor sees it is the local depot (real footage, real streets), believes it is safe and fast (Supergasbras, trained installers, open now), taps WhatsApp for gas or water to the depot nearest them.
FIRST VIEWPORT (as built): Ochre field. Left: headline at step-5 condensed black, lead, live delivery status list (gás/água), secondary "Ver os depósitos" tag-link + quiet phone link. Right 5 columns: the live order ticket (signature) on a riveted galvanized label, orange seal "Enviar no WhatsApp" as the primary action. Under it a stamped ribbon (GLP · Revenda Supergasbras · Desde 2003 · cidades). The real footage moved to a full-bleed band directly below, tagged "P13 · 13 kg GLP · Revenda Supergasbras". Adaptations: no kicker above the headline (craft-floor ban); no tare figure (a P13 tare is not 13 kg, false claim); no ticket serial (would read as a real order number). ORIGINAL PLAN: Ochre field. Left 7 columns: stamped kicker line "GÁS · ÁGUA MINERAL · DESDE 2003", headline "Acabou o gás? A água? A gente leva." at ~clamp(3.2rem,8vw,7.5rem) condensed black; live status tag "Aberto agora · entrega de gás até 21:30"; primary orange seal button "Pedir pelo WhatsApp" + secondary "Ver depósitos". Right 5 columns: portrait-cropped looping real footage in a label frame with stamp "P13 · TARA 13,0 KG". Footer strip of the viewport: four depot names as a stamped ribbon.
SIGNATURE: Order ticket — choose Gás or Água, choose depot (with neighbourhoods), live open/closed from hours, compose WhatsApp message; opens wa.me. Motion grammar: stamp-in (short scale+opacity press) on markings, nothing floaty.
FORM: Candidate 4 of 7 (cylinder & jug markings / technical notation); seed key 1a387639 (degraded roll, no challengers).
